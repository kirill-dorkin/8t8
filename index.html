FAQ

Is this live coaching?

No. It is self-paced and designed to work without calls or scheduled mentoring.

Do I need to be an advanced developer?

No, but you should know basic JavaScript concepts and be comfortable editing code.

What will I build?

A small full-stack portfolio app with a real database, authentication and a live deployment.

Why only 7 days?

The goal is momentum and completion. You can take longer, but the material is structured as seven focused missions.

Is the anime coding companion an AI tutor?

Not in this first version. She is a visual guide and course companion. We are keeping the product simple so the focus stays on shipping your app.
